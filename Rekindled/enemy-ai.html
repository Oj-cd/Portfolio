Enemy AI Design

Designed the Gargoyle enemy's four state AI using a lightweight state machine with weighted random intent selection, a hide behavior that uses other enemies as cover, and a low health retreat that forces the Gargoyle to stop attacking.

State Machine

The Gargoyle (a flying bat enemy) runs four states through a SimpleStateMachine (a lightweight state machine utility in the project). All transition predicates are defined as lambda closures in OnEnable so the conditions sit next to the states that use them.

  • Patrol: wanders between waypoints. Transitions to Chase when the player enters detection range.
  • Chase: closes distance to the player using NavMesh pathfinding. Transitions to Attack within strike range or Hide when the intent system evaluates to hide.
  • Attack: three phase coroutine: charge at the player, telegraph pause, claw activation, then retreat to the preattack position. Circle direction alternates each attack to vary approach angle.
  • Hide: moves behind a nearby Golem (a large armored enemy) relative to the player's position. When health drops below hidingLifeThreshold, intent is forced to Hide and attack is suspended.
Weighted Random Intent

The Gargoyle does not always attack when it could. A weighted random intent system reevaluates on an interval so it sometimes prioritizes hiding even when the player is in range, preventing completely predictable behavior.

  • An EnemyIntentStruct list in the inspector assigns a weight to each intent (Attack, Hide, Patrol). Higher weight means more likely to be chosen on any given evaluation.
  • Intent is reevaluated every intentCheckInterval seconds, not every frame. This means the Gargoyle commits to its current behavior for a window before reconsidering.
  • Selection is proportional: the sum of all weights is computed, a random value is drawn in [0, sum), and the winning intent is the one whose cumulative weight crosses that value.

Normal state: weighted random

All three intents compete normally. The Gargoyle might choose to hide even at full health, making it feel like a thinking enemy rather than a direct charge bot.

Low health: forced hide

When health drops below hidingLifeThreshold, intent is overridden to Hide every evaluation. The attack coroutine is cancelled and the Gargoyle retreats to cover immediately.

Hide Behavior

The Gargoyle uses the Golem enemy as cover. It specifically moves to a position behind the Golem relative to the player's angle so the Golem's body shields it from the player's line of sight.

  • Every updateCheckInterval seconds, OverlapSphereNonAlloc scans for a nearby Golem. If multiple Golems are in range, the closest one is chosen.
  • The hide position is computed by taking the direction from the player to the Golem, extending it by a configurable offset behind the Golem, then calling NavMesh.SamplePosition to find a valid walkable point at that location.
  • If no Golem is found, the Gargoyle falls back to maximum distance retreat along its current heading.

Why NavMesh.SamplePosition instead of going directly to the computed point? The geometric hide position may be inside the Golem's collider or off the NavMesh entirely. SamplePosition finds the nearest valid walkable point within a search radius, so the Gargoyle always arrives somewhere navigable.

Attack Sequence

The attack is a three phase coroutine: a charge, a telegraph pause, then the actual hit window. The telegraph gives the player a visible window to react.

  • Phase 1, Charge: MoveSpeedMultiplier is boosted and the Gargoyle accelerates toward the player's position. The target position is snapped at charge start so the player can dodge sideways if they react in time.
  • Phase 2, Telegraph: movement stops for previewDuration seconds. Duration is configurable so designers can tune difficulty independently of other values.
  • Phase 3, Claw: the claw trigger collider activates for the hit window then deactivates. After the claw phase, the Gargoyle retreats to its preattack position.
  • Circle direction alternation: each attack stores whether the Gargoyle circled left or right. The next attack flips it so the Gargoyle approaches from a different angle every other attack.
BatEnemyMovement

The Gargoyle's passive movement is handled by BatEnemyMovement, a component that runs a sinusoidal wing bob and a polar coordinate orbit around its current target.

  • Wing bob: increments local Y position each frame and reverses direction at configurable min and max bounds. Speed and amplitude are separate fields so the bob can be tuned independently of flight speed.
  • FMOD wing beat SFX plays on a timer interval, not tied to animation events, so the sound fires even in states where the animator is not running a wing cycle.
  • CircularMovement orbits the player target using polar coordinates: the angle offset increments at orbitSpeed degrees per second each frame, and the world position is set to target + (sin(angle), 0, cos(angle)) * radius. No NavMesh needed for orbiting.
